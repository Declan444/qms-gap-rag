# MDR reference notes (paraphrased)

Paraphrased from Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR) and the current harmonised standard list, for testing.
Check against the official text on EUR-Lex before relying on any of it.

## MDR replaced the Medical Devices Directive
Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR) replaced Council Directive 93/42/EEC (MDD). Quality system documents for devices certified under the MDR should reference the MDR, not the MDD.

## Article 10(9)(b) and Annex II - General safety and performance requirements
Manufacturers must identify which general safety and performance requirements (GSPRs) in Annex I apply to each device, and document the solutions adopted to meet them, with justification for any that do not apply. This forms part of the technical documentation under Annex II, Section 4.

## Annex I Section 3 - Risk management
Manufacturers must establish, implement, document and maintain a risk management system as a continuous process throughout the device lifecycle. The current harmonised standard is EN ISO 14971:2019 (with amendment A11:2021). ISO 14971:2007 has been superseded.

## Article 61 and Annex XIV - Clinical evaluation
Manufacturers must plan, conduct and document a clinical evaluation, including a clinical evaluation plan, in accordance with Article 61 and Annex XIV Part A. The clinical evaluation must be updated throughout the device lifecycle with data from post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF) under Annex XIV Part B, which requires a PMCF plan, or a justification where PMCF is not considered necessary.

## Article 27 - Unique Device Identification
Manufacturers must assign a UDI to the device and all higher levels of packaging before placing it on the market, place the UDI carrier on the label, and keep the UDI database information up to date.

## Article 15 - Person responsible for regulatory compliance
Manufacturers must have within their organisation at least one person responsible for regulatory compliance (PRRC) with the required expertise. Micro and small enterprises may instead have such a person permanently and continuously at their disposal.

## Articles 83 and 84 - Post-market surveillance system and plan
Manufacturers must plan, establish, document, implement, maintain and update a post-market surveillance system proportionate to the risk class of the device, based on a post-market surveillance plan.

## Article 85 - Post-market surveillance report (Class I only)
Manufacturers of Class I devices prepare a post-market surveillance report summarising results and conclusions of the analysis of post-market data, updated when necessary.

## Article 86 - Periodic safety update report (Class IIa, IIb and III)
Manufacturers of Class IIa, IIb and III devices prepare a periodic safety update report (PSUR) for each device or category of devices. For Class IIa devices the PSUR is updated when necessary and at least every two years. For Class IIb and III devices it is updated at least annually.

## Article 87 - Reporting of serious incidents
Manufacturers report any serious incident to the competent authority immediately after establishing a causal relationship, or that one is reasonably possible, and not later than 15 days after becoming aware of the incident. For a serious public health threat: not later than 2 days. For death or an unanticipated serious deterioration in a person's state of health: not later than 10 days.

## Article 87 - Field safety corrective actions
Manufacturers report field safety corrective actions to the competent authority before undertaking them, unless urgency requires immediate action.

## Article 88 - Trend reporting
Manufacturers report any statistically significant increase in the frequency or severity of incidents that are not serious incidents, or of expected undesirable side-effects, that could significantly affect the benefit-risk analysis.

## Articles 10(9)(j) and 89 - Communication
The quality management system must handle communication with competent authorities, notified bodies, other economic operators, customers and other stakeholders, including providing information and documentation to competent authorities on request.
