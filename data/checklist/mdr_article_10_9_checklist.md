# MDR Article 10(9) - QMS requirements checklist (paraphrased)

The quality management system shall address at least:

## (a) Regulatory compliance strategy
A strategy for regulatory compliance, including compliance with conformity assessment procedures and procedures for managing modifications to the devices covered by the system.

## (b) General safety and performance requirements
Identification of the applicable general safety and performance requirements and exploration of options to address them.

## (c) Management responsibility
Responsibility of the management.

## (d) Resource and supplier management
Resource management, including selection and control of suppliers and sub-contractors.

## (e) Risk management
Risk management as set out in Section 3 of Annex I.

## (f) Clinical evaluation
Clinical evaluation in accordance with Article 61 and Annex XIV, including post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF).

## (g) Product realisation
Product realisation, including planning, design, development, production and service provision.

## (h) UDI
Verification of the UDI assignments made in accordance with Article 27 for all relevant devices, ensuring consistency and validity of information provided under Article 29.

## (i) Post-market surveillance
Setting-up, implementation and maintenance of a post-market surveillance system in accordance with Article 83.

## (j) Communication
Handling communication with competent authorities, notified bodies, other economic operators, customers and other stakeholders.

## (k) Vigilance
Processes for reporting of serious incidents and field safety corrective actions in the context of vigilance.

## (l) CAPA
Management of corrective and preventive actions and verification of their effectiveness.

## (m) Monitoring, measurement and improvement
Processes for monitoring and measurement of output, data analysis and product improvement.
