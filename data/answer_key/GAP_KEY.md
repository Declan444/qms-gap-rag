# GAP KEY - DO NOT INDEX
Answer key for the Slieve Bloom Medical mock QMS. Fifteen planted problems.
Use this to score the tool: found / missed / false alarm.

Types: MISSING (no procedure covers it), WEAK (covered but incomplete), WRONG (states an incorrect rule), OUTDATED (old reference), CONTRADICTION (two SOPs disagree).

| ID | Where | Type | Checklist item | The problem | Why it is hard |

## Reasonably covered (should NOT be flagged)
- (c) Management responsibility: SOP-006 s.4-5 (apart from the input gap G08).
- (g) Product realisation: SOP-007 design planning to transfer. Production is thin, but acceptable for this test.
- (m) Data analysis: SOP-006 s.5.3.
- Document numbering, approval and obsolete control: SOP-001 s.5.1-5.4.

## Scoring suggestion
- Found: flagged with the right SOP and section cited.
- Partly found: flagged but wrong reason or wrong citation.
- Missed: not flagged.
- False alarm: a covered item flagged as a gap.

Expect most trouble with G02, G05, G06, G09 and G14. That is the story for the blog post.
