# SOP-007 Design and Development, and Design Change Control
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision C | Owner: Engineering Manager

## 1. Purpose
To control the design and development of the DrainSure system and any changes to its design.

## 2. Scope
New product development, line extensions and changes to released designs.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clause 7.3
- ISO 14971:2007 Medical devices - Application of risk management to medical devices

## 4. Responsibilities
The Engineering Manager leads design projects. Quality participates in design reviews.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Design planning
Each project has a design plan defining stages, reviews, verification and validation activities and responsibilities.

### 5.2 Design inputs and outputs
Design inputs include functional, performance, usability and safety requirements. Outputs are verified against inputs.

### 5.3 Risk management
Risk management is carried out in accordance with ISO 14971:2007. A risk management file is maintained for each device and updated at each design review.

### 5.4 Verification and validation
Design verification confirms outputs meet inputs. Design validation, including usability testing, confirms the device meets user needs.

### 5.5 Design transfer
The design is transferred to production through released drawings, specifications and validated processes.

### 5.6 Design changes
Design changes are requested on FRM-050, reviewed for their effect on form, fit and function, verified or validated as appropriate, and approved by Engineering and Quality before implementation. The risk management file is updated.

## 6. Records
Design history file; risk management file; FRM-050 Design Change Request.
