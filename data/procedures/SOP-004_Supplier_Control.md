# SOP-004 Supplier Control
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision A | Owner: Purchasing Manager

## 1. Purpose
To ensure purchased materials and services meet specified requirements.

## 2. Scope
Suppliers of raw materials, components, packaging and sterilisation services for the DrainSure system.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clause 7.4

## 4. Responsibilities
The Purchasing Manager selects and monitors suppliers. Quality approves new suppliers.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Selection
New suppliers complete a supplier questionnaire (FRM-030). Suppliers holding ISO 9001 or ISO 13485 certification are approved on receipt of a copy of their certificate.

### 5.2 Approved Supplier List
Approved suppliers are entered on the Approved Supplier List. Purchase orders may only be placed with listed suppliers.

### 5.3 Purchase orders
Purchase orders state the part number, revision, quantity and any certificate of conformance required.

### 5.4 Incoming inspection
Goods are inspected on receipt against the purchase order and certificate of conformance.

### 5.5 Monitoring
Supplier delivery and quality performance is reviewed once a year by the Purchasing Manager.

## 6. Records
FRM-030 Supplier questionnaire; Approved Supplier List; incoming inspection records.
