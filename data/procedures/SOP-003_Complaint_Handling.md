# SOP-003 Complaint Handling
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision B | Owner: Customer Service Lead

## 1. Purpose
To ensure customer complaints about the DrainSure system are received, recorded, investigated and closed consistently.

## 2. Scope
All written or verbal communications from customers, distributors or users alleging deficiencies in identity, quality, durability, reliability, usability, safety or performance of a device.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clause 8.2.2
- SOP-002 CAPA

## 4. Responsibilities
Customer Service logs complaints. The Quality Manager reviews and classifies them.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Receipt
Complaints are logged on FRM-020 within two working days of receipt, with customer details, lot number and a description of the problem. Returned product is quarantined.

### 5.2 Review
All complaints received during the month are reviewed together at the monthly quality meeting, where the Quality Manager decides whether an investigation is needed.

### 5.3 Investigation
Where required, the complaint is investigated by Engineering or Production. Retained samples and the device history record are examined.

### 5.4 CAPA
If the investigation finds a systemic cause, a CAPA is raised under SOP-002.

### 5.5 Response and closure
The customer receives a written response. The complaint is closed when the investigation is complete and the customer has been informed.

## 6. Records
FRM-020 Complaint form; complaint log.
