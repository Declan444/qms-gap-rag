# SOP-006 Management Review
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision B | Owner: Managing Director

## 1. Purpose
To review the quality management system at planned intervals to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness.

## 2. Scope
The whole quality management system of Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clause 5.6

## 4. Responsibilities
The Managing Director chairs management review and is accountable for the quality management system. The Quality Manager prepares the inputs.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Frequency
Management review is held twice a year, and additionally when a major quality issue arises.

### 5.2 Inputs
The review considers: customer feedback and complaints; audit results; process performance and product conformity; status of CAPAs; follow-up actions from previous reviews; and supplier performance.

### 5.3 Data analysis
The Quality Manager presents trend charts for complaints, nonconformities, scrap rates and on-time delivery, and highlights adverse trends.

### 5.4 Outputs
Outputs include decisions on improvements to the quality system and processes, product improvements, and resource needs. Actions are assigned with owners and dates.

### 5.5 Quality objectives
Quality objectives are reviewed and reset at each management review.

## 6. Records
Management review minutes and action log, retained for at least ten years.
