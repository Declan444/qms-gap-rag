# SOP-005 Training and Competence
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision B | Owner: HR and Quality

## 1. Purpose
To ensure personnel performing work affecting product quality are competent on the basis of education, training, skills and experience.

## 2. Scope
All employees and contractors whose work affects the quality of the DrainSure system.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clause 6.2
- SOP-001 Document Control

## 4. Responsibilities
Line managers identify training needs. HR maintains training records.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Induction
New starters complete induction covering the quality policy, GMP basics and their job-specific procedures.

### 5.2 Training matrix
A training matrix lists the procedures each role must be trained on.

### 5.3 Procedure revisions
When a procedure is revised, affected staff must complete training on the new revision within 30 days after its effective date.

### 5.4 Effectiveness of training
The effectiveness of training is evaluated by a short quiz or observed task, recorded on FRM-040.

### 5.5 Records
Training records are retained for the duration of employment plus five years.

## 6. Records
FRM-040 Training record; training matrix.
