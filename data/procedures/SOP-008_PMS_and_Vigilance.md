# SOP-008 Post-Market Surveillance and Vigilance
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision B | Owner: Quality Manager

## 1. Purpose
To collect and review experience gained from devices on the market, and to report serious incidents and field safety corrective actions to the competent authority.

## 2. Scope
The DrainSure single-use wound drainage system, Class IIa, sold in the EU.

## 3. References
- Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR), Articles 83 to 92
- SOP-003 Complaint Handling
- SOP-002 CAPA

## 4. Responsibilities
The Quality Manager maintains the post-market surveillance plan and submits vigilance reports to the HPRA.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Post-market surveillance plan
A post-market surveillance plan defines the data sources: complaints, distributor feedback, literature searches, similar-device recalls and the EUDAMED database.

### 5.2 Data collection and analysis
PMS data is collected continuously and analysed every six months for trends.

### 5.3 Post-market surveillance report
The Quality Manager prepares a Post-Market Surveillance Report (PMS Report) once a year summarising the results and conclusions of the analysis, and any preventive and corrective actions taken. The clinical evaluation report is updated as needed.

### 5.4 Serious incident reporting
Where a complaint may be a serious incident, the Quality Manager assesses it. Serious incidents are reported to the HPRA within 30 calendar days of the company becoming aware of them.

### 5.5 Field safety corrective actions
Field safety corrective actions are notified to the HPRA before they are undertaken, together with a field safety notice for customers.

### 5.6 Trend reporting
Statistically significant increases in non-serious incidents are reported as trend reports.

## 6. Records
PMS plan; PMS Report; vigilance reports; field safety notices.
