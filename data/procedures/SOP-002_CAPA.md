# SOP-002 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision B | Owner: Quality Manager

## 1. Purpose
To eliminate the causes of nonconformities and potential nonconformities and prevent their recurrence.

## 2. Scope
Nonconformities arising from complaints, internal and external audits, production, suppliers and post-market surveillance.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016, clauses 8.5.2 and 8.5.3
- SOP-003 Complaint Handling

## 4. Responsibilities
Any employee may raise a CAPA. The Quality Manager assigns an owner and approves closure.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Initiation
CAPAs are raised on FRM-010 and logged in the CAPA register with a unique number.

### 5.2 Investigation and root cause
The CAPA owner investigates using 5 Whys or a fishbone diagram and records the root cause.

### 5.3 Action plan
The owner proposes corrective and/or preventive actions with target dates. The Quality Manager approves the plan.

### 5.4 Implementation
Actions are implemented and evidence (revised documents, training records, photos) is attached to the CAPA record.

### 5.5 Closure
Once all actions are complete and evidence is attached, the Quality Manager signs off the CAPA as closed.

### 5.6 Trending
Open CAPAs are reviewed at the monthly quality meeting. CAPA numbers are reported to management review.

## 6. Records
FRM-010 CAPA form; CAPA register.
