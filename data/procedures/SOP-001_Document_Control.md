# SOP-001 Document Control
Slieve Bloom Medical Ltd | Revision C | Owner: Quality Manager

## 1. Purpose
To define how quality system documents are created, reviewed, approved, issued, changed and withdrawn, so that only current, approved versions are in use.

## 2. Scope
All controlled documents in the quality management system: quality manual, procedures, work instructions, forms, specifications and drawings for the DrainSure wound drainage system.

## 3. References
- EN ISO 13485:2016 Medical devices - Quality management systems
- Council Directive 93/42/EEC concerning medical devices (MDD)
- SOP-005 Training and Competence

## 4. Responsibilities
The Quality Manager maintains the document register and approves all procedures. Department heads review documents affecting their area.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Document numbering
Each document receives a unique number (SOP-xxx, WI-xxx, FRM-xxx) and a revision letter starting at A.

### 5.2 Review and approval
New and revised documents are reviewed by the author's manager and approved by the Quality Manager before release. Approval is recorded on FRM-001 Document Change Request.

### 5.3 Training before release
Before a new or revised procedure becomes effective, all affected staff must complete training on it. The effective date is set only after training records are complete.

### 5.4 Distribution and obsolete documents
Approved documents are published on the shared drive in read-only PDF. Superseded versions are moved to the Archive folder and marked OBSOLETE. One copy of each obsolete document is retained for the lifetime of the device plus two years.

### 5.5 External documents
Standards and regulations are held in the External Documents folder. The Quality Manager checks for new versions once a year.

## 6. Records
FRM-001 Document Change Request; Document Register; training records under SOP-005.
